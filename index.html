Karnataka Rural Connectivity Index

“Bridging the Last Mile: Transforming Rural Isolation into Economic Growth & Life-Saving Access Across Karnataka.”

A specialized decision-support platform for infrastructure planners to prioritize last-mile rural road construction. By combining empirical economic ROI analytics, APMC agricultural mandi access, emergency hospital transit times, and KSRTC public bus mobility, the platform transforms raw geographic data into actionable infrastructure investment roadmaps.

📌 Four Pillars of Rural Infrastructure Evaluation
🌾

APMC Agricultural Access

Eliminating post-harvest crop spoilage by reducing travel times to APMC Agricultural Mandis (Tur, Paddy, Cotton, Tomatoes).

🏙️

Urban Jobs & Higher Education

Slashing commute delays for rural youth to regional smart cities, garment parks, industrial corridors, and technical institutes.

🏥

Emergency Hospital Transit

Dramatically reducing emergency ambulance transit times to District ICU Hospitals for maternal care and life-saving medical needs.

🚌

KSRTC Bus Access & Shakti Yojna

Unlocking Gramina Sarige bus frequency and enabling free KSRTC bus pass travel for rural women, school students, and senior citizens.
